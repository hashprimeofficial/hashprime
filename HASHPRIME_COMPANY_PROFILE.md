## 🎨 Brand Identity & Presentation Rules

### 1. The Executive Visual Style
*   **Primary Background:** Deep Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0A`). Represents solidity, prestige, and institutional strength.
*   **Brand Accent:** Hash Gold (`#D4AF35`). Represents premium value, quality engineering, and growth.
*   **Secondary Accent:** Parchment Gold (`#F5E0A3`). Used for gradients, highlights, and active states.
*   **Typography:** Headings in **Space Grotesk** (demands attention, geometric, modern) and body text in **DM Sans** (clean, professional, readable).

### 2. AI Presentation Support Rules (For Owner's Tone)
*   **Owner's Voice:** Confident, strategic, multi-sector, growth-oriented. Speak of "dependability", "integrated services", "diversified assets", and "years of trusted delivery".
*   **Rule on Technical Jargon:** Omit security details (2FA, SSL, encryption, cold storage) and tech jargon. Instead, describe it as *"Institutional Risk Mitigation"*, *"Sovereign Asset Diversification"*, or *"Operational Security Standards"*.
*   **Clean Slide Design:** Max 3-4 key bullet points or a balanced 2-column comparison structure. Use numbers as big focal points.
